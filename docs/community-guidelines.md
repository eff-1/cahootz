# Cahootz — Community Guidelines & Academic Integrity Policy

> **STATUS: PILOT BASELINE (`MODERATION_POLICY.md` Chapter 12)**

---

## 1. Core Values
1. **Respect Your Study Partner's Time**: If an emergency prevents you from continuing a session, use the **Consented Emergency Exit** button rather than leaving your partner waiting.
2. **Keep It Academic**: Course communities and direct messages exist to help classmates master course material, solve past questions ethically, and stay accountable.
3. **Zero Tolerance for Harassment**: Unwanted romantic advances, bullying, tribal/religious slurs, threats, or sharing private contact details without consent result in immediate restriction or suspension.
4. **Academic Honesty**: Discussing concepts, past questions, and problem-solving methods is encouraged. Posting live exam questions during an examination or paying others to complete coursework is strictly prohibited.

## 2. How to Protect Yourself
- **One-Tap Block**: Blocking any user immediately hides your profile from them, prevents them from inviting or messaging you, and removes them from your discovery feed.
- **One-Tap Report**: Reporting a message, post, or study session captures an immutable evidence snapshot for moderator review.
- **Visibility Controls**: You can toggle **Discoverable** off at any time in your profile settings when you want to study solo.
