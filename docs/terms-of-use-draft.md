# Cahootz — Terms of Use (DRAFT)

> **STATUS: DRAFT — NEEDS COUNSEL REVIEW (`OPEN-06`, `OPEN-07`)**
> This draft sets out the student terms for the closed pilot of Cahootz. Every legal clause requires review by qualified Nigerian legal counsel prior to public release.

---

## 1. Eligibility & Academic Purpose
1. You must be at least **16 years old** and enrolled in (or preparing for courses at) a supported higher-education institution.
2. If you are aged **16 or 17**, your account operates in **Restricted Safety Mode** (institution-only visibility, direct messaging restricted to confirmed study-session partners). *Parental/guardian consent mechanics require final counsel sign-off (`OPEN-06`).*
3. Cahootz is strictly an **academic study-commitment and peer-learning platform**. It is not a dating service, anonymous chatroom, or exam-cheating marketplace.

## 2. Fair Study Commitment & Accountability
1. When you accept a Study Together invitation, you commit to showing up on time and studying honestly for the agreed duration.
2. **Fairness First**: Cahootz will never require you to turn on a webcam or share your screen to prove participation, and we will never penalize your reliability score for power cuts or mobile network drops within the reconnect grace window or via our 24-hour fairness dispute review.

## 3. XP, Streaks & Virtual Coins (No Monetary Value)
1. Experience Points (**XP**), **Streaks**, and **Coins** (when enabled) are non-monetary gamification records stored in an append-only ledger.
2. **No Sale, Transfer, or Cash-Out**: Virtual currency cannot be purchased with real money in the MVP, cannot be transferred between users, and can **never** be redeemed or cashed out for Naira or any other currency.
3. Essential academic features (partner discovery, Study Together sessions, and course communities) will always remain free of purchase requirements.

## 4. Academic Integrity & Acceptable Conduct
You agree **not** to:
- Share leaked examination papers, live exam answers, or contract-cheating services.
- Harass, stalk, dox, or send unsolicited non-academic messages to other students.
- Attempt to manipulate session timers, forge heartbeats, or enumerate other users' accounts.

## 5. Enforcement & Appeals
Violations may result in content removal, temporary restrictions (24 hours or 7 days), or account suspension. Every moderation sanction may be appealed once; appeals are reviewed by a different moderator than the one who issued the original sanction.
